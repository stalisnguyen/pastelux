The project integrates a full range of amenities for daily life and leisure, with a multifunctional garden as the central feature: swimming pool, outdoor kitchen, basketball court and tropical landscaping reflecting an Asian family lifestyle. Interior spaces take the opposite register — refined, minimalist, prioritising clarity and comfort.
